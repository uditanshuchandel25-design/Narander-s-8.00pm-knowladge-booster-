    1) Open https://app.netlify.com/drop on your PC.
    2) Drag and drop ONLY the "public" folder from inside this folder onto the page.
    3) Netlify gives you a live https://....netlify.app link immediately.
    4) Send that link to the Android phone on WhatsApp -> open in Google Chrome.
    5) Tap ⋮ (top-right) -> "Install app" (or tap "Install App" in the System tab) -> Install.
    6) Your Red "BM" icon installs to the Home Screen & App Drawer!

  ON IPHONE (SAFARI):
